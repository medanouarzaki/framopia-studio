# Block 7 — do the generated images fill their own canvas?

Generated by `npm run image-fill`. **Read-only**: no image was regenerated, the cutout
sidecar was not re-run, and every file measured was already on disk.

## The definitions

- **cutout content** — non-zero alpha. The matte is what the Block 4 quality gate already
  accepted as the subject, so nothing new is decided here.
- **original content** — colour whose largest per-channel difference from the mode's
  background `#1A0000` exceeds 24 of 255. The mode's own style fragment places the
  subject "lit against #1A0000", so the ground is a stated colour rather than a guess.
  Thresholds of 16 and 40 were measured too and are reported below, because a single
  threshold on a deliberately dark image should not go unqualified.
- **content fraction** — the box's short edge over the canvas's short edge, as asked.
  Where scaling is concerned the **long** edge is what binds, and it is reported beside it.

## The verdict on the hypothesis

**Confirmed — but not by the route the hypothesis proposed, and the correction is worth
making because it changes what to fix.**

*Not* by the route proposed: the files mostly **do** fill their own canvas. Originals reach
a median **1.000** of the long edge, and only 2 of 8 fall below 0.9. Only
2 slot renders from a cutout at all, so the cutout column is two numbers
(0.681 and 1.000) and not a distribution — no median should be read from it.

Confirmed all the same, one level down: what is small is **the subject inside the
picture**, measured from each file's own matte. It occupies a median **0.701** of the
long edge and as little as **0.548**. Fitting the file to the square therefore fits the
scene, and the bottle inside the scene arrives at a fraction of it.

On top of that, **both image templates put `IMG_MAIN` at 1000 inside a 1200 comp**, losing
a further 0.167 of every placed square before a pixel is drawn.

Multiplied out, the worst slot shows its subject at **0.567** of the square it was
given. **The effective on-screen size of every image has been overstated by roughly that
factor for the whole block**, and no figure published before this one accounted for it.
## What the builder scales by

`service/src/build/reel-plan.ts`:

```ts
export function placeholderScalePercent(options: {
  auditedSolidWidth: number;
  auditedScalePercent: number;
  sourceWidth: number;
}): number {
  return (auditedSolidWidth / sourceWidth) * auditedScalePercent;
}
```

`sourceWidth` comes from `imageSize()`, which reads the PNG IHDR or the JPEG frame header —
**the canvas, not the content**. So the answer to "canvas or content" is canvas, and
session 4's 1000/2048 was a canvas ratio throughout.

## Presentation, and a mismatch found on the way

**1 slot(s) carry a template built for the other presentation**: vitasilk img004 is `card` on `img_slide_left`. Template assignment is a seeded shuffle over the mode's allowed variants and does not read `presentation` — the quality gate sets that later. A card rendered in the cutout template simply has no frame around it; nothing fails, and nothing said so until now.
