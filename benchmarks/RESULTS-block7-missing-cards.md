## 3. Why each word has nothing readable on screen

**Nothing is skipped.** `buildReel` drops a card only for a missing `templateId`, missing
display timing, or a missing file — never for being short. Every card below is placed in
the comp. The reason it cannot be read is that its whole life is shorter than its
entrance: `sub_pop` animates opacity 0 to 100 and a blur to zero over `introS` 0.13 s,
which is 3.9 frames, and a card on screen for 0.040 s is 1.2 frames. It never reaches full
opacity, so it registers as a faint smear rather than a word — which is indistinguishable
from absence, and is what was reported.
