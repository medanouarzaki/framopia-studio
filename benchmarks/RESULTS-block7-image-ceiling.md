# Block 7 — what each image constraint is worth

Generated by `npm run image-ceiling`. **Read-only**: no constant was changed and no
placement was written. Each row relaxes one thing and holds everything else at today's
value; the last row relaxes all of them.

The first five rows keep the **zone rectangle**, which is how a placement is made today.
The last three drop it — it was a conservative device for finding free space, never a
product rule — and bound the square by the mask, the subtitle band, the bottom exclusion
and the frame instead.

## Caveats

- The mask rows use the mask's **bounding box** unioned over the frames the slot is on
  screen, not its silhouette. A box is conservative wherever a head is not square, so
  those rows are a floor on the true ceiling rather than the ceiling itself.
- `SCALE_JITTER` does not move the ceiling — it varies the realised side either side of
  `FILL_FRACTION`, so removing it removes a downside of up to 0.080 rather than raising
  the maximum.
- The zone rows are bounded by the zone, which is derived from the **person** mask, not
  the head mask. Hair-versus-face therefore changes nothing while the zone is in force —
  it only matters once the zone is dropped.
