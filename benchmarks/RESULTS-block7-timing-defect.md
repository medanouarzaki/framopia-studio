## 3. `sourceText` is off by one, and it is not the cause

Every word's `sourceText` names the *next* word's draft token: `w0006` is
`text: minutes` / `sourceText: يمكن`, and `يمكن` is `ymkn`, which is `w0007`.

| reel | words | sourceText differs from text | of those, equal to the **next** word's text |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ground truth | 76 | 71 | 4 |
| test 1 | 67 | 62 | 3 |
| test 2 | 69 | 66 | 7 |
| test 3 | 58 | 51 | 15 |
| vitasilk | 73 | 71 | 9 |

The cause is in `service/src/transcription/plan-builder.ts`: the field is documented
as "the draft word the corrected word anchored to" but is assigned
`draftWords[i]?.text` — **a positional index into a different array**. The correction
pass inserts words (Block 3 measured 15 insertions across 291 production words), so
from the first insertion onward the two lists no longer share an index.

**It is cosmetic.** `sourceText` is provenance and nothing reads it — not grouping,
not display timing, not the builder. The rendered text and the timings both come from
other fields. It is a real defect and it should be fixed, but it is not what the user
saw.

## 6. Diagnosis

**Best supported: the second word of a two-word card is on screen before it is
spoken, and around 4 s the cards are short enough that it dominates.**

Evidence for:

- Every word in the span carries a Scribe interval for the same word, so the audio and
  the text agree about *when* each word happens.
- The anticipation is real and large: pooled median **0.410 s**, max **0.870 s**.
- In the reported span the cards are 0.36–0.78 s of speech, so an anticipation of
  0.33–0.51 s is a large fraction of the card's life — the reading eye is ahead of the
  ear for most of the time the card is up.

Evidence against, stated because it is real:

- **This is not specific to 4 s.** It happens on every two-word card on every reel, and
  vitasilk has worse cases later (`g023` at 0.830 s). If the user perceived something
  *particular* to 4 s, anticipation alone does not explain why there and not elsewhere.
- A second thing happens in the same span and could be what was seen: `w0012` "li" is
  0.080 s of speech and `w0013` "ghayrdd" is 0.020 s. Two cards flash through in under
  a fifth of a second between 4.259 and 4.699, with a 0.34 s hole in the speech before
  the second. A card that flashes reads as mistimed even when its timing is exact.

**The data does not separate these two.** Both are present in the same 0.5 s and both
would produce "the text is out of step". Naming one would be a guess.

**Neither is a defect of this block.** The word durations come from Scribe and the
alignment that carries them; grouping and the builder reproduce them faithfully. The
0.020 s and 0.080 s words are a transcription-quality question — Block 2 territory —
and `findShortWords` has been reporting them since Block 3 without anyone acting.
